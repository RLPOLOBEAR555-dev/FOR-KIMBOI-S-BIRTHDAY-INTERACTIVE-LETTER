The Letter I Hid Between the Pages

To the girl who found the hidden page,

If you're reading this, then somehow you made it through the little labyrinth I hid behind an ordinary envelope.

I suppose that is fitting.

There are parts of a person that are easy to see, and there are others tucked quietly between the pages—little thoughts, little fears, little hopes, waiting for someone patient enough to discover them.

And I hope that even after all the letters, all the clues, and all the mysteries are gone, we never stop discovering those hidden pages in each other.

Not because love needs to solve every mystery,
but because I still want to know you.

I want to know the stories you haven't told yet. The dreams you haven't named yet. The versions of you that haven't even existed yet.

So this tiny egg wasn't hiding a prize.

It was hiding one more reminder:

I would still choose to find you,
even if I had to wander through every shelf in the world.

Happy birthday again, Kim. ♥

Still finding you,
Rod Lawrence
